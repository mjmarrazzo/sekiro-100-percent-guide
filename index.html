Intro

This is a Walkthrough that will lead to 100% Achievement Completion while grabbing every dialogue and important item possible. The reason for the "Intended" is that this will lead through the game in the most natural way available. What I mean by this is that certain events only happen before or after other events while certain items can be used in special ways in certain areas.

To give an example, the Divine Abduction Tool can be used to kill monks instantly, so it should be grabbed before encountering monks, but you should go to the monk area before killing a certain boss otherwise you miss interacting with an entire NPC. The "efficient" way of doing things would lead to missing the NPC altogether while the "intended" way will require backtracking now and again so that every interaction is made.

I will also be doing my best to guide through this game while spoiling as little of the games surprises as possible. That said, I will be merely hiding spoilers and easy strategies for bosses behind spoiler tags, so they are still available should you want to see them.

If you have any questions or feedback, let me know in the comments and I hope you enjoy the guide.

Ending Decision Part 1

From here you will be given a choice. Obey the Iron Code or Break the Iron Code.

If you obey, the game will end and you will get the Shura Ending. If you refuse then the game will continue.

I believe the game is "intended" to be completed with the endings in the following order.

Immortal Severance > Shura > Return > Purification.

The reason for Purification being after Return is that, despite the extra steps in Return and many others opinion that it’s the most “complete”, I’d argue Purification feels more like a “True” Ending for the story as it is the only ending in which a very important thing happens that really feels like it ends the story. So I personally recommend finishing the endings using the above order to get the most satisfaction from the experience.

The rest of this guide from here will be under the assumption that you are following in the order of endings listed above but if not then you can scroll down towards the bottom to see my tips for the Shura Ending.

Upgrade All Prosthetic Tools

Most tools are self-explanatory and easily upgraded with money as you will have enough resources within 2 playthroughs to buy everything. The outliers are that you will need x4 Lapis Lazuli and likely will need some extra Fulminated Mercury.

Unlock All Skills

Simple, grind out the experience. It takes longer the more levels you get so don’t bother buying attack with skill levels as it’s not worth it.

The more you go through NG+, the more experience you get so save grinding for your last run. You will also get bonus experience for giving Kuro his charm at the beginning of any NG+. As long as you never turn the difficulty back down, this effect will last you all game but will also make all enemies harder. So it’s a question of will it save you time or will it make enemies waste more of your time and that’s a personal decision.

The best grind spot is at the Upper Tower - Antechamber Idol during the final invasion.

This is tedious so save this as the last thing you want to do as you’ll have plenty of opportunity to not do this.
